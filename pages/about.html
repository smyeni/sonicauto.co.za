About Sonic Auto

At Sonic Auto, we believe every vehicle deserves quality parts, and every customer deserves honest advice.

Founded by an engineer with a passion for reliable motoring, Sonic Auto was created to help motorists, workshops and fleet owners source dependable automotive parts with confidence. We understand that the right component doesn't simply fix a problem—it restores performance, safety and peace of mind.

Our focus is on supplying carefully selected automotive products, including engine oils, filters, cooling system components, drive belts, ignition parts, service items and other quality replacement parts from trusted manufacturers.

We value technical understanding just as much as product quality. Rather than simply selling parts, we strive to help customers make informed decisions based on their vehicle's requirements and intended use.

Our Principles

  • Quality products from trusted brands.
  • Honest technical guidance.
  • Fair pricing.
  • Reliable service.

Whether you're maintaining your family vehicle, operating a workshop or managing a fleet, Sonic Auto aims to become your trusted automotive parts partner.
